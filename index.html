ⓘ Understanding the Licensing Change

  • Old model (Pre-2024): Pay once for perpetual license per CPU socket + annual Support & Subscription (SnS) renewal (~20-25% of license cost). You "own" the license forever.
  • New model (Broadcom, 2024+): Subscription-only per physical core. No perpetual option. Minimum 16 cores billed per socket even if CPU has fewer. Two bundles: VCF (full stack) and VVF (vSphere + vSAN only).
  • VVF (vSphere Foundation): Includes vSphere Enterprise Plus + vSAN Enterprise. Replaces buying these separately.
  • VCF (Cloud Foundation): Includes vSphere + vSAN + NSX + Aria Suite. Full SDDC stack as a single subscription.
  • Key impact: Customers with high core-count CPUs (32+ cores) see the biggest cost increase. Customers who already licensed NSX + vSAN separately may find VCF competitive.
